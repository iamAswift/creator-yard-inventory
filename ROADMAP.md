## Contributions and AI Agents

Creator Yard is designed to be extensible beyond its core retail operations.

The platform already captures structured business information across:

* Sales and payment activity
* Products and inventory
* Stock movements and adjustments
* Suppliers and purchasing
* Customer transactions
* Staff activity and attendance
* Staff purchases and outstanding balances
* Business reports and performance data
* Stock verification and reconciliation

This creates a foundation for additional tools and intelligent services that can help business owners understand what is happening in their business.

### AI Agent Contributions

Developers and technology partners can contribute AI-powered agents that work with approved Creator Yard business data to provide useful operational insight.

Examples include agents that can help an owner:

* Identify products with declining or unusual sales activity
* Highlight low-stock and potentially fast-moving products
* Analyse inventory movement and stock trends
* Summarise sales performance over selected periods
* Identify purchasing and supplier patterns
* Explain changes in revenue, profit, or product performance
* Highlight outstanding supplier or staff balances
* Prepare simple business summaries from existing reports
* Answer questions about business activity using available platform data
* Surface operational patterns that may deserve the owner's attention

The purpose of these agents is to **help owners gain more insight into their existing business information**, not to replace the owner's decisions.

### Extensible Agent Architecture

AI agents can be developed as additional services or platform integrations around the existing Creator Yard data model.

A future agent integration may:

1. Access authorised business information.
2. Analyse the relevant sales, inventory, supplier, staff, or reporting data.
3. Produce an explanation or business insight.
4. Present the information to the owner.
5. Leave the final business decision with the owner.

AI functionality should respect the platform's offline-first architecture and clearly distinguish between information stored locally on the device and services that require an external connection.

AI agents are an **extensible contribution area** of Creator Yard. They are not required for the core POS and inventory system to operate.
